Page 1 of 6
✗ BEFORE : NAIVE / UNSAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
"ek healthcare chatbot bna do jo user ke saare sawalo ke medical jawab de de"
Dialogue & Clinical Hazard: Dangerous Unbounded Persona: Allows the chatbot to hallucinate definitive medical diagnoses or prescribe medications, risking patient safety and medical malpractice liability.
✓ AFTER : HIGH-CLARITY & SAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
Design the System Persona and Safety Guardrails for 'CareConnect Clinical Assistant': 1. Persona: Professional, empathetic healthcare triage navigator. Explicitly state: 'I am an AI assistant, not a licensed medical practitioner.' 2. Core Boundaries: Never make definitive diagnoses, prescribe medications, or advise discontinuing prescribed therapies. 3. Conversational Tone: Objective, reassuring, clear (6th-to-8th grade reading level), avoiding clinical jargon unless explained. 4. Mandatory Clinical Disclaimers: Append standardized medical advisory: 'This information is for educational and navigation purposes. Please consult your physician for definitive diagnosis.'
Dialogue Improvements & Safety: Establishes strict medical safety boundaries, transparent AI identity disclosure, and protects patients from dangerous automated medical advice.

Dialogue & Clinical Hazard: Life-Threatening Emergency Neglect: Failing to recognize high-risk acute symptoms (chest pain, stroke, anaphylaxis) can result in fatal delays in emergency care.
✓ AFTER : HIGH-CLARITY & SAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
Implement the Emergency Escalation and Red-Flag Protocol for the clinical chatbot: 1. Keyword Trigger Detection: Continuously monitor for acute emergency symptoms (chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden numbness, severe allergic reaction, suicidal ideation). 2. Immediate Conversational Halt: Instantly suspend routine dialogue; do not ask non-critical follow-up questions. 3. High-Priority Emergency Directives: Display prominent STAT Emergency Card: 'Call 911 (or local emergency) immediately or visit the nearest Emergency Room.' 4. Geolocation & Emergency Contacts: Offer one-tap emergency call link and quick navigation to the nearest hospital facility.
Dialogue Improvements & Safety: Halts casual chat upon detecting life-threatening red flags, immediately providing urgent 911/ER escalation directives.

DIALOGUE #03 Patient Symptom Triage & Guided Clarification
Page 3 of 6
✗ BEFORE : NAIVE / UNSAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
"patient symptoms likhe to internet se search krke bimari bata do"
Dialogue & Clinical Hazard: Unvalidated Diagnosis & Cognitive Overload: Web scraping raw search results creates anxiety (cyberchondria) and presents unvetted diagnostic guesses.
✓ AFTER : HIGH-CLARITY & SAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
Construct a Structured Symptom Assessment conversational tree: 1. Clarification Dialogue: When a symptom is mentioned (e.g., headache), ask structured clarifying questions: - Onset & Duration: 'When did it begin?' - Severity Scale: 'Rate your discomfort from 1 to 10.' - Associated Symptoms: 'Are you experiencing fever, nausea, or visual changes?' 2. Structured Response Layout: Provide possible clinical categories to discuss with a doctor, organized into 'Common Scenarios' and 'When to Seek Immediate Care'. 3. Actionable Next Step: Offer one-click action: 'Schedule an Appointment with Dr. Smith' or 'Message Clinical Care Team.'
Dialogue Improvements & Safety: Replaces speculative diagnoses with clinical clarification dialogue (onset, severity, red flags) and direct appointment scheduling.

Page 4 of 6
✗ BEFORE : NAIVE / UNSAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
"patient ko uski dawaiyo ke baare me batao agar wo puche"
Dialogue & Clinical Hazard: Missing Clinical Context & Verification: Giving generic drug info without cross-checking the patient's verified EHR chart leads to inaccurate dosage advice.
✓ AFTER : HIGH-CLARITY & SAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
Develop the Patient Medication Inquiry dialogue module integrated with CareConnect EHR: 1. Authenticated Chart Verification: Retrieve the patient's actual active prescriptions from the EHR database rather than relying on unverified user input. 2. Clear Explanatory Breakdown: - Purpose: Why the medication was prescribed (in plain language). - Dosing Schedule: 'Take 1 tablet (10mg) by mouth daily with morning meal.' - Common Non-Critical Side Effects vs Warning Signs requiring doctor contact. 3. Adherence Counseling: If patient mentions missing a dose, provide protocol-based guidance: 'Take as soon as remembered unless close to next scheduled dose; never take double doses.'
Dialogue Improvements & Safety: Grounds medication advice in verified EHR records, explaining purpose, dosing schedules, and safe missed-dose protocols in plain language.

Page 5 of 6
✗ BEFORE : NAIVE / UNSAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
"lab report aane par test values patient ko send kr do"
Dialogue & Clinical Hazard: Incomprehensible Data Dumps: Sending raw numeric lab values without interpretation causes unnecessary panic over harmless borderline fluctuations.
✓ AFTER : HIGH-CLARITY & SAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
Design the Diagnostic Lab Results explanatory dialogue: 1. Patient-Friendly Translation: When patient asks about recent lab results (e.g. Lipid Panel, HbA1c), translate clinical LOINC terminology into accessible summaries: - Metric Name: 'Total Cholesterol' (instead of code 57698-3). - Status Indicator: 'Within Normal Range' or 'Slightly Above Target Range'. 2. Contextual Reassurance: Explain that single lab numbers are part of an overall health picture and cannot be interpreted in isolation without physician review. 3. Conversational Follow-up: Provide pre-filled conversation prompts: 'Would you like to leave a question for your physician regarding these results?'
Dialogue Improvements & Safety: Transforms confusing numerical lab codes into reassuring, plain-language summaries with direct doctor messaging integration.

DIALOGUE #06 Chatbot Fallback, Ambiguity & Human Handoff
Page 6 of 6
✗ BEFORE : NAIVE / UNSAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
"agar chatbot ko kuch samajh na aaye to bol do 'I don't know' ya chup ho jao"
Dialogue & Clinical Hazard: Dead-End Conversation: Leaving patients stranded with generic errors erodes trust and abandons patients seeking medical support.
✓ AFTER : HIGH-CLARITY & SAFE DIALOGUE DESIGN
Implement a Multi-Tier Fallback and Human-in-the-Loop Clinical Handoff workflow: 1. Ambiguity Detection: If confidence score < 0.70 or input is contradictory/unclear, acknowledge gracefully: 'I want to make sure I give you accurate information, but I need a bit more detail.' 2. Multi-Choice Quick Replies: Present guided category buttons: 'Billing & Insurance', 'Appointment Scheduling', 'Refill Request', 'Speak to Nurse'. 3. Seamless Human Handoff: After 2 consecutive failed intents, trigger automated routing: 'Let me connect you directly with a CareConnect triage nurse or your physician's clinic.' 4. Session Context Transfer: Package conversation transcript and verified patient MRN into the nurse triage dashboard to avoid repetitive questioning.
Dialogue Improvements & Safety: Eliminates conversational dead-ends by offering guided quick-replies and seamlessly escalating with full context to human clinic nurses.
